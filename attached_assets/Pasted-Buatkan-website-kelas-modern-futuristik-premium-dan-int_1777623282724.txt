STYLE UTAMA:

- Gunakan konsep "like iOS app design + modern SaaS dashboard"
- Gunakan animasi halus seperti "Framer / Apple website"

- Gunakan glassmorphism + blur + transparansi

ANIMASI:

- Smooth transition (fade, slide, scale)
- Hover efek glow + elevate

- Scroll reveal animation
